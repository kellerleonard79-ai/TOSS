About TOSS

We love Mardi Gras, and catching beads is part of the magic. But after the parade rolls by, many of those plastic beads end up in landfills, storm drains, and our waterways, where they never break down. Around New Orleans alone, that adds up to 25 million pounds.

So that's why we founded TOSS Inc. (Throwables Offering Sustainable Solutions), a Pensacola nonprofit that keeps the fun and leaves out the plastic. We give krewes biodegradable beads for free, so the sustainable choice is as easy to grab as the plastic one.

All of our work is funded by donations from people who love this tradition as much as we do. Together, we're showing that Gulf Coast tradition and environmental responsibility can coexist, one parade at a time.

Our partner

TOSS works with Louisiana State University's PlantMe Beads program, which develops biodegradable alternatives to traditional plastic Mardi Gras throws.

LSU supplies the beads, and TOSS uses donor funding to get them to krewes in Pensacola and across the Gulf Coast at no cost to the krewes. Together, we're making biodegradable beads as easy for krewes to get as plastic ones.
